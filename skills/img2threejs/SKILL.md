# Img2ThreeJS Scene Direction

## Purpose
Use image-driven 3D or visual scene generation deliberately, only where it creates real differentiation for the LP.

## Why This Needs Restraint
Three-dimensional elements can elevate a page quickly, but they can also make the experience noisy, slow and visually disconnected from the offer.

## Use This Skill When
- The hero needs a signature visual object.
- A product concept benefits from dimensionality or spatial metaphor.
- The LP needs one memorable interactive or ambient scene.

## Do Not Use When
- The page already has weak strategic clarity.
- The scene exists only to signal technical sophistication.
- Mobile performance is already fragile.
- The team has no plan for graceful fallback.

## Scene Selection Rules
- One primary scene is usually enough for a landing page.
- The scene should reinforce the promise, not distract from it.
- The visual metaphor must be explainable in one sentence.
- The 3D asset should live inside the brand system, not outside it.

## Technical Direction
- Isolate scene logic from content sections.
- Provide fallback presentation for unsupported or lower-power contexts.
- Reduce unnecessary interactivity unless it improves understanding.
- Balance texture, lighting and movement for readability.

## Design Rules
- Avoid over-detailed 3D assets that dominate the copy.
- Keep camera behavior controlled and calm.
- Match motion tempo between the 3D scene and the rest of the page.
- Use 3D to create presence, not chaos.

## Deliverable Format
